date: 2026-10-05
---

Initially contrived in the mind of @cowfartcop as a reaction to the governor's call to action, this organization was created as a non-profit tech incubator as a way to help curious, talented and capable Arkansans hone and apply their skills

Our approach is based in the open source ethos, and grounded in the notion that humanity is not a zero sum game. By learning from each other, and joining together to do that which is useful to others, our future improves just as certainly as our present
